# Arte de Shadow Fest

Modo: herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes. Arte conceptual, no fotografía de un evento realizado.

Archivos: `assets/horror-hero.png` (máster), `assets/horror-hero.webp` (versión de uso web). Decoraciones nativas: `assets/blood-edge.svg` y `assets/festival-icons.svg`.

Prompt final usado:

> Create a premium cinematic website hero artwork for SHADOW FEST 4.0, a Halloween reggaeton and bellakeo nightclub festival in Lima. Landscape wide 1536x1024 composition. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO LOGOS, NO WATERMARKS. Right half: an enormous sculptural bone-white cracked demon carnival mask with curved chrome horns, dark eye sockets glowing dim crimson, glossy theatrical fake-blood streaks flowing over the mask and dripping down the jaw. No wounds, no injured person, no body horror anatomy; it is a physical theatrical mask art installation. Rich 3D photographic realism, meticulous materials, luxury horror club poster art direction. Around the mask chrome chains and twisting tentacles of electric violet light, fine red suspended liquid droplets, red smoke, rough distressed black textures and crimson laser haze. Lower right faint silhouettes of adult dancers in a nightclub, not explicit. Very deep near-black burgundy background, moody dramatic red rim light, beautiful purple specular reflections. Left 45 percent MUST remain almost black quiet negative space for a huge HTML headline, fine subtle blood spatters only on extreme outer edges. Mask entirely on the right and not cut off; strong high-end graphic hierarchy, cinematic depth, maximum visual impact, sophisticated rather than cartoon, not stock photo. Asset will be integrated as a web hero, not a screenshot of a website.
